Marlow District Council
Minutes of the Full Council meeting held on Thursday 2 April 2026

Item 7: Kingfisher Footbridge

7.1 The Director of Infrastructure reported that the bridge had been closed to the public on 14 March 2026 following receipt of the Halden Associates inspection report dated 10 March 2026.

7.2 Members considered two options: Option A, a temporary repair to cables C2 and C3 at an estimated cost of £450,000 with an expected life of five years; and Option B, full replacement of all four cables and reconstruction of the west anchorage drainage, at an estimated £1.8 million.

7.3 The Leader of the Council said that a temporary fix would be throwing good money after bad unless the drainage was also corrected, and that on balance the council should proceed with the full replacement.

7.4 Councillor Reeve asked whether a shuttle bus could be provided between the station and the market square while the bridge was closed. The Director of Infrastructure said that officers were in discussion with the bus operator and hoped to have a service running by 1 June 2026.

7.5 RESOLVED (9 votes to 3, with 2 abstentions): that Option B be approved, with works to commence in September 2026 subject to procurement.
