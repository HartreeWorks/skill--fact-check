Marlow Gazette, 9 April 2026
Traders count the cost as Kingfisher bridge stays shut

Market square traders say the closure of the Kingfisher footbridge has cut their takings by about 20 percent, as commuters and shoppers arriving at the station on the east bank no longer cross to the west bank.

"The station side has never been busier and we have never been quieter," said Anita Kowalski, who runs the fruit stall. Several traders said customers were staying on the east bank, where a new cafe and a convenience store have opened near the station.

The council voted last week to replace all four of the bridge's cables at a cost of £1.8 million, with work due to begin in September. A council spokesperson said a shuttle bus between the station and the market square was expected to be running by 1 June.

The bridge was closed on 14 March after engineers found corrosion in two of its cables.
