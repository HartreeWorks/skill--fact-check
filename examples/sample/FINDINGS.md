# Fact-check findings: The Marlow footbridge closure

Review in the page: open `review.html`. Hover a highlight for the source passage; click to pin and record a decision. "Problems only" shows the items below.

Totals: 28 claims. 10 supported, 9 supported with a caveat, 4 contradicted, 3 unsupported, 2 opinion or forecast.

Sources: Halden Associates inspection report (10 March 2026), Marlow District Council minutes (2 April 2026), Marlow Gazette (9 April 2026). All three were available as text; every quote below was re-found in them by string search.

## Fix before publishing

1. **"published on 12 March 2026"**: the report is dated 10 March 2026. (c005)
2. **"two days after the report was published"**: 14 March is four days after 10 March. The arithmetic only works with the wrong date. Say "four days after". (c011)
3. **"foot traffic has moved from the east bank to the west bank"**: the Gazette says station arrivals on the east bank are no longer crossing to the west; the direction is reversed. Say "foot traffic has stayed on the east bank". (c015)
4. **"promised a shuttle bus by 1 May"**: minutes and Gazette both say 1 June, and the council "hoped" or "expected", not promised. (c017)
5. **"Nobody has yet inspected the others"**: no source says so; Halden recommends inspection, which is not the same. (c020)
6. **"becoming more frequent"** and **"the fastest-growing problem in local transport budgets"**: no source. Cut, or source them. (c021, c022)

## Wording that outruns the source

7. **"a temporary fix would be throwing good money after bad"**: the minutes attach "unless the drainage was also corrected", and record it as reported speech rather than a verbatim quote. (c014)
8. **"about 3,000 pedestrians a day"**: the report gives an average of 2,900 crossings, 4,100 on market days. (c003)
9. **"voted 9 to 3"**: with 2 abstentions. (c012)
10. **"estimated the replacement at £1.8 million"**: excludes VAT and traffic management, and covers drainage works too. (c009)
11. **"built across England"**: the report says "under the county bridge programme"; "across England" is not in the source. (c018)
12. **"since at least 2019, when a previous inspection first noted the problem"**: the 2019 inspection noted standing water; nothing says it was the first to. (c010)
13. **"work to start in the autumn"**: September, "subject to procurement". (c013)
14. **"has been shut ever since"**: the sources confirm closure only as of 9 April. (c901)

## Opinion presented as fact

- "Councils should inspect every bridge of this design before the end of the year" is the author's recommendation; the report recommends inspection without a deadline. (c023)

## Process

Two fast-model verifiers (14 and 13 claims), one Claude adversary run with the verifier output withheld, quotes audited by string search against the three source files, reconciled and adjudicated by the lead model. The three planted errors in this sample (date, direction, trimmed quote) were all caught by both the verifiers and the adversary.
